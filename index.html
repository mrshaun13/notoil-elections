      What it means

      What is the Senate?Half of Congress: 100 members, 2 per state, 6-year terms, about a third elected every 2 years. It passes laws and approves judges, cabinet picks, and treaties.

        Seat counts

        What is this?Seats per party before and after. D = Democrat, R = Republican, I = Independent. Called = a news desk like the AP projected the winner (not yet certified).

        Governor

        "Up" = on the ballot this year. "All" = the whole Senate (100) or all governors (50). The 2 independent senators vote with Democrats and were not up in 2026.

        Seats that flipped

        What is a flip?A seat that changes party, like R to D. A new person from the same party is not a flip.

        Sen* = special election (filling the rest of a term).

        Map: before and after

        What is this?One square per state. A square that changed color between the maps flipped. Senate shows the one seat per state up this year.
